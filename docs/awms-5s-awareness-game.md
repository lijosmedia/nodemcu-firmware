# AWMS / 5S Awareness Game Concept

## Working title
**5S Quest: Build the Best Workplace**

## Playable prototype
Open `docs/awms-5s-game.html` in any browser to play an interactive **2D Find & Fix** AWMS/5S game on a workplace image map (`docs/img/workplace-floor.svg`) by selecting tools and clicking issues.

## Purpose
Create a short, engaging game that helps employees **understand**, **practice**, and **apply** AWMS/5S principles at work, home, and shared spaces. The game should make 5S practical, memorable, and repeatable.

## Target audience
- Plant and office employees
- Contract staff
- New joiners (onboarding)
- Team leaders and supervisors

## Format options
1. **Mobile/web game** for individual play and leaderboard
2. **Team challenge mode** for toolbox talks / shift meetings
3. **Physical + digital hybrid** using printed cards and QR scans

---

## Core gameplay loop
Each player/team progresses through 5 levels (1S to 5S). In every level:
1. Identify issues in a messy virtual workplace scene
2. Make decisions under time and budget constraints
3. Execute actions aligned to that S
4. Get instant feedback and points
5. Unlock the next S only after meeting minimum compliance score

### Scoring framework
- **Correct action**: +10
- **Best-practice action**: +20
- **Wrong action / safety risk**: -15
- **Time bonus**: up to +25 per level
- **Sustainability bonus** (in 5S level): +50 for consistent daily practice streaks

### Reward mechanics
- Badges: *Red Tag Champion*, *PEEP Master*, *Zero-Dirt Detective*, *Checklist Guardian*, *5S Role Model*
- Team leaderboard by department
- Monthly “5S Awareness Star” recognition

---

## Level design mapped to each S

## Level 1: **Re-Organise (Seiri)**
**Learning goal:** segregate useful vs non-useful items and remove clutter.

### Mini-games
- **Red Tag Rush:** drag unnecessary items to Red/Yellow Tag area before timer ends.
- **Seiri Museum Builder:** place obsolete items into a museum/archive and justify why.

### Win criteria
- Correctly classify at least 80% of items
- Free up required floor/storage space

---

## Level 2: **Neatness (Seiton)**
**Learning goal:** place necessary items for fast retrieval and visual order.

### Mini-games
- **PEEP Puzzle:** assign every tool/item to a fixed, labeled location.
- **Golden 30 Seconds:** retrieve requested tools within 30 seconds.

### Win criteria
- Everything has a marked place
- Retrieval time reduced by predefined target

---

## Level 3: **Cleanliness (Seiso)**
**Learning goal:** clean and inspect proactively (Macro -> Individual -> Micro approach).

### Mini-games
- **Spot the Dirt & Defect:** identify dirt, leaks, loose bolts, and unsafe conditions.
- **3-Step Clean Cycle:** choose correct sequence for macro, individual and micro cleaning.

### Win criteria
- Clean all hotspots
- Report all abnormalities correctly

---

## Level 4: **Standardisation (Seiketsu)**
**Learning goal:** convert good practices into repeatable standards.

### Mini-games
- **Checklist Builder:** create daily/weekly checklists from observed tasks.
- **Visual SOP Match:** match photos with correct procedure cards.

### Win criteria
- Complete standard checklist with no critical misses
- Achieve uniform procedure compliance across virtual departments

---

## Level 5: **Discipline (Shitsuke)**
**Learning goal:** build habits and culture through consistent self-discipline.

### Mini-games
- **Habit Streak Challenge:** maintain 5S activities over simulated 30-day cycle.
- **Peer Coach Decisions:** choose best responses for real behavior scenarios.

### Win criteria
- Maintain consistency over time
- Demonstrate ownership: “Do it yourself, Do 5S daily”

---

## Engagement features
- **Daily micro-challenges** (2-3 minutes)
- **Department vs department battle weeks**
- **Scenario packs**: workshop, office, warehouse, pantry, home study
- **Multi-language support** for workforce diversity
- **QR-triggered real-world missions** (example: scan your area and submit before/after photo)

## Real-world adoption bridge
To ensure behavior transfer from game to workplace:
1. Link each level to one real action task in actual workplace
2. Manager validates completion in AWMS checklist
3. Award in-game bonus points for verified implementation
4. Display monthly improvements (space saved, retrieval time, audit scores)

## Suggested implementation roadmap (8 weeks)
- **Week 1-2:** Finalize content, scenarios, language, scoring
- **Week 3-4:** Build prototype (Level 1 & 2)
- **Week 5:** Pilot with one department
- **Week 6:** Add Level 3-5 + leaderboards
- **Week 7:** Integrate feedback and AWMS checklist mapping
- **Week 8:** Company-wide launch + communication campaign

## Success metrics (KPIs)
- Participation rate (% employees completed all levels)
- Average assessment score by S level
- Reduction in retrieval time for tools/documents
- Improvement in 5S audit scores
- Number of verified workplace/home implementation submissions
- Repeat engagement (weekly active users)

## Optional campaign tagline ideas
- “Play 5S Today, Perform Better Every Day.”
- “Clean. Organize. Standardize. Sustain.”
- “From Game to Habit: 5S Everywhere.”
